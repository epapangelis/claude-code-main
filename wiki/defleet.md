# Defleet

**Summary**: Initiative to define and systematize the process for flagging a car for end-of-fleet-life and all the team handoffs, actions, and system changes that follow.
**Context**: [instacar]
**Sources**: instacar_claude_project_instructions.md
**Last updated**: 2026-04-15

---

## Problem

When a car reaches the end of its fleet life, the process for handling it -- flagging it for sale, coordinating team handoffs, updating system statuses -- is not clearly defined. This initiative aims to formalize that process.

---

## Scope

The defleet process involves multiple teams:
- **Ops** -- identifying cars ready for end-of-life
- **Sales** -- picking up the car for resale (used car sales vertical)
- **ARM** -- ensuring any outstanding maintenance is resolved before defleet
- **Finance** -- TBD

---

## Status

Active initiative. Process definition and team handoff mapping in progress.

Note: The subscriptions data model shows a "defleeted" vehicle stage and "fordefleet" reservation type, indicating system-level support exists but workflow formalization is needed. (source: subs export.csv)
