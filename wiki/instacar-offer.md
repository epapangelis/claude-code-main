## What It Is

When a customer is close to converting, a Sales agent sends them a personalised offer by email. The offer includes:
- Vehicle details and pricing
- Subscription/lease terms
- A link to my.instacar.gr where the customer uploads their financial documents

Document upload at my.instacar.gr is one of two paths -- the other is replying to the booking confirmation email with attachments, which is handled automatically by [[n8n-workflow-automation]].
