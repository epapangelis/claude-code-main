# users-b2b

**Summary**: B2B small companies needing fleet vehicles — decision-makers are typically owners or office managers, not fleet specialists.
**Context**: [instacar]
**Sources**: instacar_claude_project_instructions.md
**Last updated**: 2026-04-15

---

## Profile

- Small Greek companies needing fleet vehicles (leasing or purchase)
- Decision-makers are often owners or office managers — not fleet specialists
- Value efficiency, invoicing, and account management
- Less price-sensitive than B2C but expect clear commercial terms

## Key needs

- Clear invoicing and VAT handling
- Account management — someone to call
- Efficient vehicle replacement and fleet management
- Subscription/leasing contracts with flexibility

## How they interact with instacar

- Browse and book via [[customer-facing-platform]] (B2B section)
- Managed via [[instafleet]] by Sales and CS teams
- Subscription details tracked in [[instafleet-subscriptions]]

## Design implications

- Distinguish B2B flows from B2C wherever needs diverge
- Surface VAT details and invoicing options clearly
- B2B volume means retention matters — returning customer tags exist in subscription data
