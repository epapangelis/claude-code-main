## Purpose

These FAQs are designed for landing page optimization (SEO) and customer trust-building across 14 vehicle categories. Each category targets specific search intent while maintaining consistent brand messaging.

---

## Core FAQ Pattern

Every category follows a 4-question structure:

### Q1: Why Choose [Category] Used Car from instacar?

**Core message**: Eliminates pre-owned risk through instacar's quality standards.

**Talking points**:
- "Like-new" positioning
- Fleet age: less than 5 years old
- Condition: excellent both externally and internally
- Safety: never been in accidents
- Mechanical: peak mechanical condition

### Q2: Is Technical Inspection Performed?

**Core message**: Thorough, exhaustive inspection at authorized partner service center.

**Inspection process**:
1. Structure, bodywork, interior examination
2. Mechanical and electrical components check using vehicle lift
3. For EVs/hybrids: additional battery health assessment
4. Result: only pristine-condition vehicles in fleet

### Q3: What Options/Offers Are Available?

**Varies by category**:
- Size categories: variety from city cars to SUVs/family vehicles
- Fuel type: range from small commercial to premium options
- Transmission: small to premium options
- Tier: budget to luxury models

**Consistent messaging**: "Our team will help you find the best deals tailored to your needs."

### Q4: What Payment Methods Are Available?

**Three payment options** (universal across all categories):

a) **Bank transfer** — Full one-time payment via bank deposit

b) **Santander Consumer Finance** — Financed purchase with 0% down payment, up to 84 installments (flexible terms)

c) **instacar Flexible Installments** — Direct installment plan with instacar, no bank involvement

**Trust signal**: "In every case, we handle all the bureaucracy, making the process easy and stress-free."

## Key Trust Signals

### Quality Promise
- **"Like-new" positioning**: Eliminates pre-owned purchase anxiety
- **Age guarantee**: All vehicles <5 years old
- **Condition guarantee**: Excellent externally and internally
- **Safety guarantee**: Zero accident history
- **Mechanical guarantee**: Peak mechanical condition

### Inspection Rigor
- **Partner**: Authorized service centers (credible third party)
- **Thoroughness**: Multi-step process (visual + mechanical + electrical)
- **Standards**: Battery health checks for EVs/hybrids
- **Result**: Only pristine vehicles in fleet

### Friction Reduction
- **Financing**: Three flexible payment options
- **Accessibility**: 0% down payment option via Santander
- **Term flexibility**: Up to 84 months financing
- **Bureaucracy handling**: instacar manages all paperwork

## Language and Tone

**Register**: Formal, professional, reassuring

**Greek**: Uses formal address, emphasis on reliability ("αξιόπιστο"), quality standards

## Usage & Maintenance

These FAQs are templated for SEO: each category targets distinct search intent (e.g., "used automatic cars Greece," "electric vehicles under 5 years") while maintaining consistent brand voice.

**When updating**:
- Keep the 4-question structure
- Preserve core trust signals (like-new, inspection rigor, payment flexibility)
- Vary category-specific options (Q3) to reflect actual fleet
- Always mention Santander and flexible installment payment options
- Always include bureaucracy-handling promise
