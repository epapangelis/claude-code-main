# users-b2c

**Summary**: B2C individual buyers and lessees — Greeks aged 28–55, price-sensitive, with high trust needs around vehicle condition and the online purchase process.
**Context**: [instacar]
**Sources**: instacar_claude_project_instructions.md
**Last updated**: 2026-04-15

---

## Profile

- Greeks aged roughly 28–55
- Price-sensitive — comparing against traditional dealers and private sellers
- Need strong trust signals: vehicle condition, warranties, inspection reports, financing clarity
- Often first-time online car buyers
- May be skeptical of completing a high-value transaction without in-person interaction

## Key needs

- Clear vehicle condition information (inspection reports, photos)
- Transparent pricing — no hidden fees
- Warranty and return guarantees
- Financing/leasing options explained simply
- Easy communication channel (WhatsApp, phone)

## How they interact with instacar

- Browse and discover cars on [[customer-facing-platform]]
- Receive offers via email (see [[instacar-offer]])
- Sign leasing/subscription contracts
- Interact with CS team via [[instafleet-subscriptions]] post-delivery

## Design implications

- Lead with trust signals on every key page
- Keep pricing transparent and upfront
- Default to Greek-market dynamics — not US/UK product playbooks
- Avoid jargon; use plain language
