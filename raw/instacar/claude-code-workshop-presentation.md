### Full Post

We built a Claude Code workflow that lets our product team go from idea to spec and decision in half the time. Here's what it took.

**Why it matters**

Faster specs = faster engineering. Faster decisions = fewer weeks debating trade-offs. But the real win is compound: teams ship more, learn more, ship again. Knowledge stacks instead of evaporating.

**How**

Three things glued together:

1. **LLM wiki** — accumulated product knowledge lives in one place. Claude reads context from a year of decisions, not scattered docs. New person onboards differently. Old decisions don't get re-litigated.

2. **MCP servers** — we wired Claude into Figma, Linear, Slack. Context flows in. Spec references your design system live. Links to open tickets. Team conversation history. No context-switching.

3. **Design tokens in instafleet** — we injected our internal tool's design language into Claude. Every spec respects the system automatically. No "but will this work in instafleet?" three iterations later.

These three things alone don't matter. Together they collapse time.

**What's next**

This is year one. Workshop last week was to show the team how to think in this model. Next: scaling across teams, making this the default way we do product.
